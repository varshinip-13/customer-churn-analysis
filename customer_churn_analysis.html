Retention recommendations

Fix onboarding drop-off
58% of churned users skipped onboarding. Add interactive checklists, contextual tooltips, and a 7-day activation email sequence tied to feature milestones.
High impact
Re-engagement triggers
Auto-flag accounts with no login in 7 days. Send personalized win-back campaigns at day 10; offer a 1:1 check-in call at day 14 before risk escalates.
High impact
Drive collaboration adoption
Solo Pro users churn 2.5× more than team accounts. Prompt users to invite teammates within 72 hrs and offer a referral incentive for new seat invites.
Medium impact
Accelerate integration setup
Users with 1+ integration churn at 3.2% vs 11.8%. Add an in-app integration wizard and surface recommended integrations on the new-user dashboard.
Medium impact
Address price sensitivity
34% cite price as exit reason. Consider usage-based pricing tiers, annual plan discounts (15–20%), and a lightweight free tier as a long-term retention hook.
Medium impact
Proactive support follow-up
Users who opened 2+ support tickets show 63% churn likelihood. Route them to a CSM automatically and follow up within 24 hrs of every ticket close.
